## Comparing two different things, on purpose

This is not a normal head-to-head. A chair and a desk are complements, not
substitutes, and in an ideal world you buy both.

We are running the comparison anyway because it is the question we get asked most,
and because the way people answer it for themselves is almost always wrong. A
sit-stand desk is visible, feels like an upgrade, and is the thing you show people.
A task chair is furniture. The budget goes to the desk.

<Callout tone="method" title="What we measured, on both">
  Eight weeks with each product in daily use by two testers, one 163 cm and one 188 cm. Chair
  adjustments marked and re-measured at four and eight weeks. Desk deflection measured with a 5 kg
  lateral impulse at three heights. Both testers logged a daily discomfort score against a fixed
  scale.
</Callout>

## The discomfort log

Both testers recorded a daily score for lower back, neck, and shoulder discomfort
across the whole period, with the chair introduced in week one and the desk in week
five for one tester and the order reversed for the other.

The chair produced a clear improvement for both, in both orderings. The desk produced
no measurable change in either. That is a small sample and a soft measure, and it
matches what eight weeks of conversation with both testers suggested: the desk
changed how often they stood up, and standing up was not what had been hurting.

## Where the desk does earn its money

Once the chair is right, the Rise is genuinely good and genuinely well priced. Dual
motors, 120 kg capacity, presets that live on the controller rather than in an app,
and 1.2 mm of deflection at typing height.

Its weakness is at the top of its range — 9.4 mm of wobble at 125 cm — which matters
if you are tall, and does not if you are not.

## The order we would buy in

1. **The chair**, set up properly, in front of whatever table you have.
2. **The desk**, once you know whether you actually stand up.
3. **The lamp**, which costs a fifth of either and fixes the eye strain both of them
   ignore.

## Our answer

The Axis, if it is one or the other. The Rise is the better bargain and the chair is
the better purchase, and those are not the same thing.
