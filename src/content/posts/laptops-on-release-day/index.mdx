Every laptop we have tested in the last two years has behaved differently at
week twelve than it did at week one, and in every case the difference was in the
buyer's favour.

## What changes

Firmware is the obvious one. Fan curves get retuned, sleep bugs get fixed, and
power limits get raised or lowered once the manufacturer has telemetry from more
than a review pool. The Meridian 14 gained about forty minutes of real battery
life in its first two updates; the Vector 13 lost some peak performance and
stopped getting uncomfortably warm.

Price is the less obvious one. Nothing we have tracked has stayed at its launch
price for a full quarter.

## What does not change

Panel quality, port selection, keyboard feel, and repairability are all fixed at
launch. If a machine is wrong on any of those, waiting will not help. That is
what a review is for.

## The rule we use

Buy on release day only if the thing you need is one of the fixed properties and
the machine already has it. Otherwise, put it on a list and look again in three
months — the same laptop will be better and cheaper.
