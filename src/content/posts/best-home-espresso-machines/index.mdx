## Buy the grinder first

This is the recommendation almost everybody ignores, so it goes at the top.

A $300 grinder feeding a $400 machine makes better espresso than a $150 grinder
feeding a $1,000 machine. Every time, without exception, in nine weeks of paired
testing. The machine controls temperature and pressure, both of which matter; the
grinder controls particle distribution, which matters more, because uneven particles
mean water finds a path through the puck and the extraction is over before it has
started.

If your budget is $600 total, spend $300 on the Grind X and $300 on the cheapest
machine with a PID. If your budget is $1,100, buy both of our picks.

## The single-boiler question

The Piccolo E1 has one boiler, which means it brews at 93 C and steams at 130 C and
cannot do both at once. Going between them takes 55 seconds.

For one person drinking espresso, this is a non-issue. For a household making two
milk drinks each morning it is the thing you will complain about, and no amount of
temperature stability makes up for standing at a counter waiting for a light to
change. Dual-boiler machines start at around $1,600, and if you know you want milk
drinks for two, that is the number to plan around rather than a compromise at $749.

## What to ignore when you shop

**Bar pressure.** Every pump is 15 bar. What matters is the pressure at the puck,
which should be around 9, and whether the machine has an over-pressure valve set
correctly. Fifteen bar on a box is a marketing number.

**Pressurised baskets.** Most machines ship with one fitted. They flatter bad
grinding by creating artificial resistance, and they cap how good your coffee can
get. Replace it on day one.

**Boiler size, on its own.** A well-insulated 300 ml boiler with good PID control
outperforms a poorly managed 1.5 litre one for espresso. Size matters for steaming,
not for brewing.

**Built-in grinders.** A machine with a grinder attached is two products sharing one
chassis, and when either fails you lose both. Every combination we tested with a
separate grinder beat every all-in-one at a comparable price.
