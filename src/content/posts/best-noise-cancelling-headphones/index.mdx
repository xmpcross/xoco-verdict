## Why these two, out of fourteen

Noise cancellation has converged. Almost every pair above $200 now does something
useful, and the marketing has responded by quoting numbers that describe the
system's best moment rather than its normal one.

We measure at four frequencies and publish all of them, because the shape of the
curve is what you actually experience. A pair that attenuates 30 dB at 300 Hz and
18 dB at 100 Hz will feel worse on a train than one that manages 26 dB at both,
and the first one will have the bigger number on the box.

<Callout tone="method" title="What we did">
  Fourteen pairs between $180 and $450, all bought at retail. Each measured on a head-and-torso
  simulator across four frequencies with three runs and the pads reseated between them, then worn
  for a fortnight each on the same commute by the same two testers. Battery measured to shutdown at
  75 dB SPL.
</Callout>

## What we changed this time

The Halcyon Drift Pro is new to the list and takes the in-ear slot on sound quality
alone. We went back and forth on including it at all, because the charging case has
a real defect, and settled on including it with the warning attached — the
alternative was recommending something that sounds worse to avoid mentioning a
hinge.

The previous budget pick has been removed rather than replaced. Nothing under $180
measured well enough this round to recommend, and we would rather say that than fill
the slot.

## What to ignore when you shop

**A single decibel figure.** Ask which frequency. If the answer is not on the page,
assume it is the flattering one.

**"Up to" battery claims.** Every manufacturer measures at a volume they choose. Two
of the fourteen pairs we tested lost more than a third of their claimed runtime at a
normal listening level.

**Codec lists.** LDAC and aptX Adaptive both sound fine. Neither will rescue a
headphone with a bad tuning, and the difference between them is smaller than the
difference made by reseating the earpads.

**Head-tracked spatial audio.** Every pair here has some version of it. None of us
kept it switched on past the first week.
